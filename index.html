02 — AJ AI · In pilot with our first customer

One secure assistant that knows your whole business.

Ask "tell me everything important about ABC Windows" and get one answer drawn from your ERP and accounts (Sage 50, Sage X3, Xero), your email and files in Microsoft 365, your documents and your databases — only the parts that person is allowed to see. Not a chatbot bolted onto one system: an AI layer across the SME, built for companies of five to fifty people.

Where your data lives

Your business data is stored in the United Kingdom. AI processing takes place within the EU under Microsoft's Data Protection Addendum. Your data is never used to train any model.
  • UK stored. Database, documents, index, logs and backups in Microsoft Azure UK South.
  • EU-only AI processing. Models run inside Microsoft's EU Data Boundary; global routing is blocked by policy, not by promise.
  • Permission-aware. Every answer is trimmed to what that person may see, enforced in the database, never by the prompt.
  • Your tenant, isolated. Row-level security per customer and a full audit trail of every model call.

UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 apply. A Data Protection Impact Assessment is completed before each customer goes live. We don't call anything "GDPR compliant" because it runs on Azure; we show you the data flow.

Pilot customers get the founding price. No newsletter, just the news that matters.
