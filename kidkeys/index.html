Kid Keys
How the setup works

A keyboard that flags, not reads.

You set it up from your phone. Your child adds the keyboard on theirs. It notices risky things as they are typed and nudges them before they press send. You see categories and counts. Never messages. They see the same.

Try it on TestFlight (iPhone)

The steps, written down

    What it cannot do, so nobody is surprised

    It never reads what your child receives. Only what they type, and only to spot six kinds of risk.
    Your child can switch keyboards. If they do, you see "keyboard not used since Tuesday", not silence.
    It cannot tell which app they typed in. Apple does not give keyboards that. The optional "app opened" Shortcut fills some of that gap.
    Nothing is hidden from the child. They see the same weekly summary you do. That is the point.
