CloneWatch
How it works

Find the apps that copied yours. With proof.

Add your app once. CloneWatch searches both stores every day, compares icons, names, screenshots and descriptions, and shows you the evidence. When a copy appears you get an email and a push. The dispute pack writes Apple's and Google's forms for you.

The steps, written down

    What it will not do

    It never files anything for you. You read the evidence, you decide, you submit. A wrong accusation is yours, so the pack says "similarity evidence", never "clone" as fact.
    Features are not protected. Same idea, own words and own art is allowed. Only copied names, icons, screenshots and text count.
    Google Play has no search API. We search gently and slowly. A scan takes a few minutes, not seconds.
    An older app cannot have copied you. If theirs was released first, the pack says so and advises not filing.
