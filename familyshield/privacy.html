Family Shield

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version: Family Shield never reads your messages, calls or photos on its own. It only looks at what you choose to show it. When you check something, the text or photo is sent to our server and to an AI service to get the answer, then deleted. We keep the first 80 characters of what was checked so your family can see what was checked, and nothing else.

What we collect and why

  • What you check. When you tap Check this, the text or photo you chose is sent to our server (Supabase, hosted in London) and to Anthropic's Claude service to work out the answer. Anthropic does not keep it or train on it. We keep only the verdict and the first 80 characters, so the family phone can show "Royal Mail text: scam".
  • Pairing. The two first names you type and a six-digit code, so the two phones can find each other.
  • Alerts. A push notification token for each phone, so we can send the alert and the Sunday summary.
  • Numbers to block. Any phone number a family member adds to block.
  • A device identifier we make up, so the server can tell phones apart. It is not your Apple or Google ID.

What we do not collect

No contacts, no location, no message history, no browsing, no advertising identifiers. The call-warning and junk-text filters run on the phone and send nothing to us. We do not sell or share data with anyone except the services above that make the app work.

Who can see what

The family member who set up the app sees the verdict and first 80 characters of each check made on the protected phone. The protected person can see this is happening on the Help screen and can stop it at any time with Start again.

Keeping and deleting

Checks are kept for 90 days, then deleted. Tapping Start again on either phone removes the pairing. To delete everything, email the address below with your pairing code.

Children

Family Shield is for adults. It is not aimed at under-16s.
