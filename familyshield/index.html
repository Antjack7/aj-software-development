How the setup works

Two phones. One job: is this a scam?

You set it up from your own phone. Mum's phone gets one big button. If she finds a scam, you are told. Press play, or step through.

The steps, written down

    What it cannot do, so nobody is surprised

    It never reads anything on its own. Only what Mum chooses to show it. Texts from people in her contacts are never touched.
    It cannot listen to phone calls. No app on an iPhone can. It can warn about known scam numbers and give her a safe word.
    It needs her to take the first step. Paste, photograph or share. The double-tap and share menu make that one tap.
    Safe means "nothing we can see". When unsure it says Be careful, and the next step is always "ring your family".
    Family Shield by AJ Software. Nothing is sent anywhere unless she taps Check.
