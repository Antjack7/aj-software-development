Clarity

Dementia Clock

When you can't be there, Clarity can.

Free · No ads · No subscription, ever

Clarity is a calm, full-screen day clock for someone living with dementia, Alzheimer's or memory loss — and peace of mind for the family who love them. When the days blur together, one glance answers the questions that cause the most worry: what day is it, is it morning or night, and what is happening today.

Get Clarity on the App Store

What it shows

In big, gentle writing: the day of the week, whether it's morning, afternoon, evening or night, and the date and time — clearly and simply. The colour shifts softly through the day and dims at night, so a glance tells you roughly where you are even before you read it.

One tap to reach you

Add your name and number and a large "Call Sarah" button sits at the top of the clock — the first thing on the screen. One tap starts the call. No contacts list to scroll, no number to remember, nothing to get lost in. Choose FaceTime, FaceTime audio or an ordinary phone call, and test it during setup so you know it works before you need it.

A message from you, always on screen

Add a reassuring line only you can change — "Sarah is coming Thursday. You are safe, we love you" — so a familiar voice is there even when you can't be.

Reminders that ring, then let it rest

Set the day's reminders — tablets, meals, a visitor. When one is due it appears clearly and a gentle alarm rings, resting and trying again a few times rather than sounding all day. Your loved one taps "I've done it" and a calm done stays on screen, answering the anxious "have I already taken my tablets?". Tapped by mistake? One tap puts the reminder straight back.

Made to be trusted

  • Free — no ads, no subscription, ever
  • The clock, messages and reminders work with no internet at all
  • Nothing collected or shared — everything stays on the device
  • The screen stays on, so it's always there

Set it up in a minute on a spare iPhone or iPad, by the bed or in the kitchen. Simple for the person using it. Reassuring for the family who set it up.

Getting help

Need a hand? Email anthony@ajsoftwaredev.org and you will get a reply within two working days. There is also a step-by-step setup guide covering the call button and reminders.

Your privacy

Clarity has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Your reminders, your message and the phone number you enter never leave the device. Full details in the privacy policy.

Clarity does not diagnose or treat any condition. It is a supportive day-orientation clock and gentle reminder — not a substitute for a carer's help with medication.
