TextProof

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

The short version: TextProof runs entirely on your device. Your recordings, messages and transcripts are never uploaded anywhere, and we could not read them if we wanted to — the app has no servers, no accounts and no analytics.

What the app does with your content

TextProof reads screen recordings or screenshots that you choose from your photo library, recognises the text in them on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition, and produces a transcript. The recordings, the transcripts and any exported PDF, CSV or TXT files exist only on your device, and anywhere you yourself choose to share or save them — for example via the iOS share sheet.

What we collect

Nothing. TextProof has no server component. It does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, usage analytics, crash reports, identifiers or message content. The App Store privacy label for TextProof is “Data Not Collected”.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple through your Apple ID. Purchase state is verified through RevenueCat, a payment-infrastructure service, which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase receipt from Apple — never your messages, recordings or transcripts. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Permissions

TextProof asks for read access to your photo library solely so you can pick the recording or screenshots to transcribe. It never scans your library, and it requests no other permissions.

Your responsibilities

TextProof is designed for exporting your own conversations from your own device. You are responsible for ensuring your use of any exported material complies with the laws that apply to you.

Deleting your data

Delete an export inside the app by long-pressing it on the home screen, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Because nothing ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete on any server.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date. Questions: anthony@ajsoftwaredev.org.
