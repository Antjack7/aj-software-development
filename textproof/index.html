How it works

Three steps, and the phone does the hard one

No cable, no computer, no backup file to wrestle with. If you can record your screen, you can use this.

1

Film the chat

Open the conversation, start your iPhone's screen recording, and scroll slowly to the bottom. The app walks you through it the first time.

2

Import it

Pick the video. TextProof reads every screen, stitches the conversation back together and works out who said what.

3

Check and export

Tap any line to correct it, then export as a PDF, a spreadsheet, or plain text — or as photographs of the original screens.

Why it's different

Most apps guess. This one tells you when it isn't sure.

A record with a quiet hole in it is worse than no record at all, because nobody reading it knows. Every judgement this app makes is visible in the document.

It marks what it might have missed

Scroll too fast and it cannot promise it saw everything between one screen and the next. So it says so, in place, at the exact spot — rather than closing the gap and hoping.

Nothing is thrown away

Everything it read off your screen and decided was not a message is listed at the back of the document. If it judged something wrongly, that thing is in the wrong section. It is never gone.

It shows its working

The front page accounts for every reading taken off the screen: how many were printed as messages, how many were set aside. The numbers add up, and you can check them.

It never invents a time

Times and dates appear only where they were actually visible on screen. Where iOS did not show one, the document says so instead of guessing.

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no upload, no servers, no analytics. The reading happens on the device using Apple's own text recognition. There is nowhere for your conversation to go.

Common questions

Before you ask

Does it work with WhatsApp as well as iMessage?

Yes. It reads what is on your screen, so it works with any messaging app — WhatsApp, iMessage, SMS, anything you can film. That is the advantage of doing it this way rather than reading a backup file.

Will it print things nobody actually said?

Sometimes, and we would rather tell you now. If somebody shared a picture with writing on it — a poster, a screenshot, a photo of a letter — the app reads that writing too and cannot always tell it from a message. Tap any line you did not send to delete it before exporting. Ordinary conversations rarely need this; chats full of shared images need it most.

Can I use the export in court?

That is not ours to promise, and we will not pretend otherwise. Whether any document is accepted in any process is a decision for the people running that process. What we can say is that the export is complete, timestamped, print-ready, and honest about anything it was unsure of. Keep the original recording alongside it — that is the primary record.

Do you see my messages?

No, and we could not if we wanted to. There is no server. The app has no network code in it at all. Your recording is read on your own phone by Apple's built-in text recognition, and the result stays there until you choose to share it.

What happens when I delete a conversation?

The transcript goes, and so do the captured pictures of the conversation. Both, from the phone, permanently. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete.
